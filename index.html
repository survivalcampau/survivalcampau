Field notes

Three rules worth knowing before your first lesson.

  • Floodwater

    Never drive, ride or walk through floodwater. If it's flooded, forget it.

    NSW SES (opens in a new tab)

  • Fire danger

    Check the Fire Danger Rating before every trip. On a Catastrophic day, stay out of bush fire risk areas.

    NSW RFS (opens in a new tab)

  • Trip plan

    Tell someone your route and when you'll be back. Going remote? Carry a personal locator beacon (PLB).

    NSW National Parks (opens in a new tab)
